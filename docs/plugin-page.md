Whalepod adds coordinator chats that hand work to Executor Sessions, follow them and report the results. To start, open the Coordinator page in the sidebar and choose New coordinator.

The settings below apply at once, without a restart. The full README, with installation, configuration and security notes, is in the package (`README.md`) and in the repository, [github.com/simveit/dsh-whalepod](https://github.com/simveit/dsh-whalepod).
